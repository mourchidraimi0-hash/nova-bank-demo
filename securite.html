Security

Manage your password, your logins, and your security preferences.

NOVA BANK is a technical demonstration platform.
It does not hold any real banking license, is not certified by any security or compliance body, and does not benefit from any deposit guarantee fund. Unlike an earlier version of this site, your data (accounts, balances, cards, histories) no longer stays only in your browser: every action goes through our API and is recorded in a centralized, shared Postgres database, just like at a real bank. Your password is hashed (scrypt algorithm) before being stored — it is never kept in plain text and is never visible, even to the back-office team. Every administrative operation is logged in a server-side, append-only activity log that no one can modify or erase after the fact. This nonetheless remains a demonstration site: please never use your real banking credentials, usual passwords, or sensitive personal information here.
